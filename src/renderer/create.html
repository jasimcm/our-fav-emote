Create a character

Turn one full-body photo into a companion that walks, waves, laughs and reminds you to drink water. It takes three videos from Gemini.

Creating characters needs macOS for now.

1 Photo

Whole body, head to shoes, standing straight, arms at the sides

    Save this image, then upload it to Gemini in step 2

    2 Make three videos in Gemini

    1. Open Gemini, start a new chat, choose Video, and attach the saved image.
    2. Paste prompt 1 and send. When the video is ready, download it.
    3. In the same chat (no need to attach the image again), paste prompt 2, then prompt 3.
    Gemini Pro makes 3 videos a day: exactly enough
